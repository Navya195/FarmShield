# FarmShield Voice Diagnosis System - User Guide

## How to Use the Voice Diagnosis Feature

### Step 1: Open Voice Diagnosis
- Look for the **🎤 Microphone** button in the main navigation or dashboard
- Click it to open the Voice Diagnosis modal

### Step 2: Select Your Language
1. Click the **Language Dropdown** at the top
2. Choose from 10 languages:
   - English
   - हिंदी (Hindi)
   - తెలుగు (Telugu)
   - தமிழ் (Tamil)
   - ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada)
   - മലയാളം (Malayalam)
   - मराठी (Marathi)
   - বাংলা (Bengali)
   - ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi)
   - ગુજરાતી (Gujarati)

### Step 3: Speak Your Crop Problem (Using Microphone)
1. **Allow Microphone Access** when prompted
   - Look for the microphone icon in your browser's address bar
   - Click "Allow" for microphone permission

2. **Click the Microphone Button**
   - The button will turn RED 🛑 and show a STOP icon
   - Status shows: "🎤 Listening... Speak your crop problem now"

3. **Speak Clearly**
   - Mention the **crop name** (tomato, rice, potato, wheat, etc.)
   - Describe **symptoms** (spots, yellowing, wilting, brown patches, etc.)
   - Speak in your selected language

4. **Wait for Analysis**
   - Status will show: "🔍 Analyzing crop symptoms with AI..."
   - The spinner will indicate processing

5. **View Diagnosis**
   - AI will show:
     - **Disease Name** 🌱
     - **Confidence Score** 📊 (e.g., 92%)
     - **Severity Level** ⚠️ (Low, Moderate, High)
     - **Treatment Options** 💊 (Organic & Chemical)
     - **Recommendations** 📋

### Step 4: Or Type Your Symptoms
If microphone isn't available:

1. **Click the Text Input Field**
   - Field shows: "Or type your crop symptoms here..."

2. **Type Your Symptoms**
   - Example: "tomato plants have yellow spots on leaves"

3. **Click Send Button** or Press **ENTER**
   - Button shows: ✈️ **Send**

4. **Wait for Diagnosis** ⏳
   - Processing spinner appears
   - Results display below

## Troubleshooting

### ❌ "Microphone access blocked"
**Solution:**
1. Click the 🔒 microphone icon in your browser's address bar
2. Select **"Always allow"** for this website
3. Refresh the page
4. Try again

### ❌ "No speech detected"
**Solution:**
1. Check your microphone is working
2. Speak closer to the microphone
3. Speak more clearly
4. Try typing instead

### ❌ "Speech recognition not supported"
**Solution:**
1. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
2. Or type your symptoms instead
3. Or try Safari (on iOS/Mac)

### ❌ "Server connection error"
**Solution:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Wait a moment and try again
3. Try typing instead of voice
4. Contact support if issue persists

## Tips for Best Results

✅ **Do:**
- Speak clearly in your selected language
- Mention the crop name first
- Describe specific symptoms
- Be specific about severity
- Use crop names like: tomato, potato, rice, wheat, corn, sugarcane, cotton
- Mention symptoms like: spots, yellowing, wilting, browning, fungal, bacterial

❌ **Don't:**
- Speak too quickly
- Have background noise
- Use slang or unclear terms
- Forget to mention crop name
- Speak in a different language than selected

## How It Works

1. **Speech Recognition** - Converts your voice to text (Web Speech API)
2. **Language Processing** - Understands crop and symptoms in your language
3. **AI Analysis** - Matches symptoms to disease database
4. **Treatment Matching** - Recommends appropriate treatments
5. **Results Display** - Shows confidence scores and advice
