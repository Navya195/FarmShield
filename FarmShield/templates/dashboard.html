Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about FarmShield

How does FarmShield detect crop diseases?
FarmShield uses a deep learning model trained on 87,000+ crop images across 38 disease types. Photograph the affected leaf and the AI returns a diagnosis with 96.8% accuracy within seconds.
Does FarmShield work without internet?
Yes! FarmShield has an offline mode that caches the AI model locally. You can scan and diagnose diseases without internet. Data syncs when you come back online.
Which languages are supported for voice diagnosis?
FarmShield supports Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi, and English for voice diagnosis. More languages are being added regularly.
How accurate is the AI disease detection?
Our AI model achieves 96.8% accuracy across 38 known crop diseases. For critical decisions, always also consult an agricultural expert.
Can I share my diagnosis with an expert?
Yes, after any diagnosis you can share the full report with our certified agricultural experts via the Experts panel for personalized treatment advice.
What crops does FarmShield support?
FarmShield currently supports Tomato, Potato, Rice, Wheat, Corn, Apple, Grape, Pepper, and many more. We continuously expand our crop database with each update.

User Guide

Step-by-step instructions to get the most from FarmShield

How to Scan a Crop

1

Open One-Tap Scan

Click the camera button or select "One‑Tap Scan" in the sidebar to open the scanning interface.

2

Capture or Upload Image

Take a clear photo of the affected leaf. Ensure good lighting and the diseased area is clearly visible.

3

Wait for AI Analysis

FarmShield's AI analyzes the image in 2–5 seconds and identifies the disease with confidence percentage.

4

Review Results & Treatment

Read the detailed diagnosis: disease name, severity, organic treatments, chemical pesticides, and prevention tips.

5

Consult Expert if Needed

For critical cases, use the Experts panel to connect with a certified plant pathologist.

How to Use Voice Diagnosis

1

Open Voice Diagnosis

Navigate to Voice Diagnosis in the sidebar or tap the microphone icon in the navigation bar.

2

Tap the Microphone

Press the large microphone button. Allow microphone access when prompted by your browser.

3

Describe Symptoms

Clearly state the crop name and symptoms: e.g., "My tomato leaves are turning yellow with brown spots."

4

Get AI Advice

FarmShield's NLP engine processes your query and returns targeted agricultural advice in seconds.
